- **Tap instead of hold.** New *Show popup* setting: with *Until used*, press the hotkey once and the popup stays open until you use an item, press Esc or press the hotkey again.
- **Find snippets by name** (*Until used* only). Type in the search box at the top of the popup to list snippets and groups whose names start with those letters, from every level.
- **Side by side.** New *Popup layout* setting shows clipboard history on the left and snippets on the right at the same time. Switch sides with the mode key or ← →.
- **Edit snippets in bulk.** A new editor (right-click menu, tray menu and Settings) shows groups, slots and the selected snippet together. *Paste from Excel etc.* adds copied rows (name, contents) to the free slots of a group.
- **Easier snippet editing.** Press F2 to edit, rename or add. ↑↓ stop on empty slots, and Enter or a click there adds a snippet. The snippet dialog marks clearly whether the text or the file/folder/URI is used.
- **Indents from Outlook and Word are kept.** Paragraph indents in copied text become full-width spaces at the start of the line.
